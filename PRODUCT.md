# PRODUCT.md

register: brand

## Product Purpose

A single-event wedding RSVP website for **Aseel & Fadwa** (أصيل وفدوى), marrying **Friday, 16 Oct 2026, 7:30 PM** at **EVA Hall, Airport Road, Amman, Jordan**. Guests receive a personal invitation link over WhatsApp; the page is the digital invitation itself: it sets the mood, gives the details, and collects the RSVP (deadline 11 Oct 2026). A hidden, password-protected dashboard lets the event planner manage the guest list (Excel import/export, manual add) and track confirmations.

## Users

- **Guests** (~hundreds): family and friends in Jordan, all ages, overwhelmingly on mobile phones, opening the link from WhatsApp. Mostly Arabic speakers; some English-preferring. They will spend under two minutes on the page. It must feel like a beautiful printed invitation, not a web form.
- **The event planner**: manages the list on phone or laptop. Needs zero-training clarity: who confirmed, who declined, who hasn't answered, how many seats.

## Brand & Tone

Formal, warm, celebratory Arabic wedding-invitation register. Quiet luxury: engraved-invitation elegance, generous whitespace, slow graceful reveals. Never playful, never corporate. Arabic is the first language of the design, not a translation afterthought: the RTL layout must feel native.

Reference the client chose: the Organza demo invitation (dark olive hero, script names, cream details section, countdown, seat-capped RSVP form). Recreate the feeling and flow from scratch; do not copy assets or code.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS form styling (gray cards, blue buttons, drop shadows everywhere)
- Event-tech ticketing look (Eventbrite)
- Sparkly/glittery wedding-blog kitsch, gold-foil gradients, hearts
- Bootstrap-default admin tables for the dashboard

## Strategic principles

1. Mobile 375px is the design target; desktop is the enhancement.
2. The invitation is bilingual (ar default / en) with flawless RTL mirroring.
3. One evening, one event, no reuse ambitions: every choice can be specific to Aseel & Fadwa.
4. The RSVP form must be effortless for a 60-year-old aunt on WhatsApp.
